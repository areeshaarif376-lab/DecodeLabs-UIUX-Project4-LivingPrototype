# DecodeLabs UI/UX Internship – Project 4: The Living Prototype

## Overview

Project 4 focused on transforming static UI screens into an interactive prototype.

The objective was to create a realistic user journey that allows users to move naturally through an application while experiencing navigation, interactions, and flow before development begins.

For this project, I designed **Crisp**, an organic grocery mobile application centered around simplicity, trust, and transparency.

---

# Project Concept

## Crisp

**Fresh. Simple. Transparent.**

Crisp was designed to provide users with a straightforward way of purchasing fresh organic produce while minimizing confusion throughout the shopping experience.

The goal was not only to create visually pleasing screens but also to design an experience that feels reliable and intuitive from product discovery to order completion.

---

# Project Objective

The objective of this project was to convert static UI designs into an interactive prototype by connecting screens and creating a seamless shopping journey.

The focus areas included:

- User Flow
- Navigation
- Interactions
- Checkout Experience
- User Confidence

---

# Target Audience

Users looking for:

- Fresh produce
- Transparent pricing
- Clear product information
- A simple purchasing experience
- A fast and reliable checkout process

---

# User Flow

Browse → Product → Cart → Payment → Order Placed

This flow was intentionally designed to keep the shopping journey clear and predictable.

# Personal UX Focus

The screen I spent the most time thinking about was the **Payment Screen**.

In many ecommerce experiences, users reach checkout only to face complicated forms or unnecessary friction.

My goal was to make billing:

- Fast
- Easy
- Clear

I wanted users to spend less time dealing with payment details and more time successfully completing their purchase.

---

# Transparency In Checkout

One of the most important design decisions in this project was pricing transparency.

As online shoppers, users often dislike discovering unexpected charges at the final stage of checkout.

To reduce uncertainty, I focused on making pricing clear throughout the journey so users understand exactly what they are paying before placing an order.

This decision directly supports the Crisp brand promise:

> "What you see is what you pay."

---

# Key UX Decisions

## Clear Product Information

Product details such as pricing, quantity, calories, and organic status are visible before purchase.

## Consistent Navigation

Navigation remains familiar across all shopping screens.

## Low-Friction Checkout

Cart → Payment → Confirmation follows a predictable sequence that reduces confusion and supports completion.

## Trust Signals

Transparent pricing, organic labels, and product information help users feel confident in their decisions.
